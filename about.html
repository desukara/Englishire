About Englishire

Continuity deserves more than a hurried answer.

Englishire provides temporary English teacher cover for schools throughout Tokyo.

The service was established from practical experience of Tokyo classrooms and a simple observation: when staffing becomes difficult, schools still deserve time to make sound decisions without allowing the quality of the school day to become the price of that time.

Why Englishire Exists

The difficulty was never simply finding a teacher.

The real difficulty was finding the right teacher, with sufficient understanding, in the time available.

A teacher's absence, recruitment gap or expanding timetable may demand an immediate response. Yet availability alone does not make a teacher suitable for a classroom. The school, learners, lesson format, journey and responsibilities attached to the assignment all matter.

Englishire grew from the belief that those details should be considered before an assignment is accepted, not discovered after a teacher has arrived. Proper temporary cover gives a school room to think, recruit and plan without lowering its standards.

The school is not interchangeable
Each organisation has its own culture, routines and reasonable expectations of professional conduct.
The learner is not a timetable entry
Age, confidence, level and prior classroom experience shape how a lesson should be approached.
The assignment is not merely a vacancy
A single day, recurring timetable and extended placement demand different forms of preparation and judgement.
Urgency does not remove responsibility
The less time available, the more valuable calm and accurate judgement become.

Our Written Standard

The Standard governs the work in practice.

The Englishire Standard is the written expression of how professional teacher cover should be approached. It sets out the responsibilities that precede an assignment, the conduct expected during it and the judgement required when circumstances change.

It exists so that schools, teachers and Englishire itself may understand the principles against which each decision should be considered.

Our Responsibility

Trust should be answered with proportionate care.

Schools entrust temporary teachers with far more than a vacant hour in the timetable.

To the school

Clear communication, realistic expectations and respect for the organisation's established procedures and culture.

To the learner

Purposeful teaching, calm conduct and recognition that a change of teacher should not make the school day feel careless.

To the teacher

Sufficient information, a proper briefing and an honest account of the assignment before responsibility is accepted.
