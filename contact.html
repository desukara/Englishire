Before Sending

A clear outline is enough to begin.

  • State the school area and requested timing.
  • Describe the classes and work as clearly as circumstances allow.
  • Identify information that remains provisional or unknown.
  • Remember that no teacher is reserved until Englishire expressly confirms the assignment.
