How It Works

A clear process from first enquiry to final handover.

Schools should know what happens before cover begins.

Englishire follows a five-stage process of enquiry, review, confirmation, preparation and follow-up. Each stage removes uncertainty before it reaches the classroom.

Helpful details to include

School location, dates, timetable, learner ages and levels, class sizes, lesson format, materials and any particular requirements. Unknown details may simply be identified as uncertain.

From Enquiry to Handover

Five stages, one clear line of responsibility.

The sequence is straightforward. The judgement within it matters.

  1. 01Describe the requirement
  2. 02Review the assignment
  3. 03Confirm suitable cover
  4. 04Prepare and deliver
  5. 05Follow up and hand over

  1. 01

    The School Describes the Requirement

    The school sends a concise account of the location, dates, timetable, learners, lesson format, available materials and the reason cover is required. Urgent circumstances do not always permit every detail to be known; what matters is that known facts and remaining uncertainties are stated clearly.

    The school need not solve the staffing difficulty before contacting Englishire. It need only describe it as clearly as circumstances allow.

  2. 02

    Englishire Reviews the Assignment as a Whole

    Travel, reporting time, lesson demands, learner needs, duration, preparation time, school routines and practical feasibility are considered together. Availability is necessary, but suitability for the school and work remains the deciding standard.

    A free diary is not, by itself, a qualification.

  3. 03

    The Arrangement Is Confirmed Clearly

    Where a suitable teacher is available and the assignment can be supported properly, the dates, reporting time, location, lesson schedule, principal responsibilities and practical terms are set out clearly. An enquiry or expression of availability is not a confirmation.

    No arrangement should be treated as definite until Englishire has expressly confirmed it.

  4. 04

    The Teacher Prepares and Conducts the Assignment

    The teacher receives the timetable, reporting instructions, school contact, lesson information, learner details, materials and relevant procedures before arrival. During the assignment, material changes are communicated rather than assumed.

    Professional teacher cover begins before the classroom door opens.

  5. 05

    The Assignment Is Followed Up and Handed Over

    Relevant work completed, learner or timetable observations, outstanding responsibilities and any continuing requirement are communicated at the conclusion of the assignment. Further cover is discussed rather than presumed.

    Temporary work should end cleanly, not simply cease.

What Helps at the Outset

Useful information allows a useful answer.

Schools are welcome to send what is known and identify what remains uncertain. The following details allow the assignment to be considered with fewer assumptions and usually with greater efficiency.

Cover is confirmed only when the requirement is understood, a suitable teacher is available and the work can be supported properly.

School and contact details
School name, address, nearest station and the person responsible for the enquiry.
Dates and timetable
Requested dates, reporting time, lesson periods, breaks, finish time and movement between locations.
Learners and classes
Ages, approximate levels, class sizes and any relevant needs or circumstances.
Lessons and materials
Objectives, lesson format, materials, work already completed, homework and reporting expectations.
School procedures
Arrival, dress, conduct, safeguarding, classroom routines and communication arrangements.
Likely duration
Whether the requirement is isolated, recurring, open-ended or expected to conclude on a particular date.

What the Process Protects

Clarity for the school, preparedness for the teacher and continuity for the learner.

The School

The school should understand what has been agreed, who is arriving, what information remains required and how material changes will be handled.

The Teacher

The teacher should receive sufficient information to arrive punctually, understand the environment and carry out the work with professional confidence.

The Learner

The learner should experience a purposeful lesson and the greatest degree of continuity that temporary circumstances reasonably permit.

Suitability before convenience, preparation before placement and communication before assumption.

The process described here is the practical expression of principles set out in The Englishire Standard, which explains the wider responsibilities governing preparation, professional conduct, continuity and relationships with schools.

Practical Questions

Matters schools quite reasonably ask.

These answers concern the process itself. The fuller Questions page addresses the wider service.

How quickly will the school receive an answer?

Urgent enquiries are reviewed according to the information available. A complete location, timetable and learner description usually permit a clearer response, but no response should be treated as confirmation unless Englishire has expressly confirmed the arrangement.

What happens if some details are not yet known?

Send what is available and state what remains uncertain. Englishire can then explain which details are essential before the assignment can be reviewed or confirmed.

Does making an enquiry guarantee cover?

No. Cover depends upon the requirements, teacher suitability and availability. An arrangement is not promised before Englishire is satisfied it can be undertaken properly.

Can the arrangement change after confirmation?

Changes may be possible, though they should be discussed. A revised timetable, location, learner group or duration may affect suitability and availability.
