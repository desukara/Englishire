Why Temporary Cover Matters

Keep the school day moving while the longer-term staffing need is resolved.

A teacher becomes unwell. A new appointment takes longer than expected. A timetable expands before permanent staffing is ready. Pupils still arrive, parents still expect a proper lesson and the school must still conduct its day with composure.

Englishire gives schools time to address the longer-term staffing need carefully, without treating tomorrow's lesson as an excuse for a hurried permanent decision.

Temporary cover should relieve pressure without lowering standards.

Questions Before the Enquiry

Clear answers before a school is asked to decide.

A small selection from the full Questions reference.

How much notice does Englishire require?

More notice generally provides more options, though urgent enquiries are welcome. Availability depends upon the dates, times, location and nature of the assignment.

Can a teacher cover just one day?

Yes. An assignment may concern a single day, recurring weekly cover or a longer temporary placement.

Does making contact guarantee cover?

No. Every assignment must be reviewed before it can be confirmed.

What information should a school provide?

Please include the location, dates, timetable, learner ages, class sizes, lesson format and any particular requirements.
