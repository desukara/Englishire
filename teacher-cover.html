What Temporary Cover Means

Breathing room, properly arranged.

Temporary cover keeps lessons moving while the school resolves the wider staffing need carefully. It supports continuity without pretending that a temporary arrangement is permanent recruitment.

Temporary cover should relieve pressure without lowering standards.

Not careless substitution
Availability alone does not make a teacher suitable for a particular classroom.
Not rushed recruitment
Cover gives the school time to make longer-term appointments with proper care.
Not a lower expectation
The school's standards should not change merely because the member of staff has.
A practical interval
Proper cover preserves lessons, protects confidence and provides time for sound judgement.

Preparing for Cover

A clear briefing is one of the finest forms of welcome.

The most effective assignments begin with concise, practical information. It need not be ceremonial; it simply needs to tell the teacher what matters.

Arrival information
Building access, reporting point, contact person, start time and sign-in arrangements.
Lesson information
Timetable, rooms, materials, objectives, homework and work already completed.
Learner information
Ages, approximate levels, class sizes and any particular needs.
School expectations
Dress, conduct, classroom routines, reporting and communication procedures.

Common Questions

Before the timetable is sent.

Practical answers for schools considering temporary cover.

Can Englishire provide cover for one day?

Yes. Assignments may range from a single day to recurring or extended temporary cover, subject to suitability and availability.

How much notice is required?

More notice generally provides more options, though urgent enquiries are welcome. Availability depends upon the dates, times, location and nature of the assignment.

Does an enquiry guarantee a teacher?

No. Every requirement must be reviewed before cover can be confirmed.

What information should the school provide?

Please include the location, nearest station, dates, lesson times, learner ages, approximate levels, class sizes, lesson format, materials and any particular requirements.

When might Englishire decline an assignment?

Cover is declined when essential information is unavailable, dependable arrival is doubtful, the educational demands do not suit the available teacher or the work cannot be supported properly within the proposed terms.
